## The decision rule (pre-registered, stated before the numbers)

Pre-registered before any episode ran (ARCHITECTURE.md §8, "changing any of
this after runs exist is p-hacking"):

1. A task **passes** for an arm iff **≥2/3 seeds** pass — **≥3/5** for tasks
   re-run under escalation. `budget_kill` and `context_exhausted` count as
   failures for every arm; an `error` is re-run once and a second one stands.
2. **Success rate** = tasks passed / N. **Margin** = RLM's passed tasks minus
   the baseline's.
3. The **S4 gate passes iff the margin is ≥ +3 against ALL of B1, B2 and B3**.
   **A tie fails.** A tie-or-loss to **B2** additionally triggers the
   **pivot-to-B2** rule; a tie-or-loss to **B3** records a **pivot-to-RAG**
   finding of the same standing.
4. Every margin is reported beside an exact two-sided **sign (McNemar) test**
   over the discordant tasks and a **10,000-resample paired bootstrap CI** on
   the mean per-task fractional delta.
5. A margin landing in **{+1, +2, +3}** owes seeds **{4, 5}** on that pair's
   **discordant tasks only**; those tasks are re-decided at ≥3/5, the sign
   test and bootstrap are recomputed **once**, and both pre- and
   post-escalation figures are reported. No other recomputation is permitted.
6. Per-category results are **tabled, never gated** — §8 refuses per-category
   margin gates outright. The one per-category rule is the **zero-floor
   tripwire**: a category where RLM scores 0 while any baseline scores ≥3 of
   its tasks blocks the wording "clean pass" and names a mandatory
   category-regression finding, without touching the aggregate gate.
7. Any win claim states its **cost multiple** next to the margin.

### The finding: a verbatim-repetition attractor, seen only under the DFlash2 root

Two episodes did not converge:

- **synth-01 seed 2** (`9d9e47fb`): at turn 9 the root's cell printed the correct
  answer inside an `intersection:` set (with single-letter junk from a non-greedy
  `(.+?)`). From turn 10 to turn 78 it emitted the **byte-identical cell 70 times**,
  no prose, no `final_answer`, until C5 ended it at 90 % of the 32K root window
  (`context_exhausted`, 1,154 s). Seeds 1 and 3 solved the task in 70 s and 87 s.
- **synth-07 seed 2** (`0c1c397d`): at turn 4 a regex that requires CamelCase
  with no spaces matched nothing, so the observation was empty. From turn 5 to
  turn 115 the root emitted the **identical cell 111 times**, one every ~11 s,
  until the 1,300 s wall clock (`budget_kill`). Seeds 1 and 3: 243 s and 85 s.

Both loops start from a turn with an unchanged or empty observation, and both
episodes wrote **no prose on any turn from turn 1**; the passing seeds of the
same tasks wrote a sentence before most cells. At temperature 0.7, 70–111
byte-identical 600-character continuations is not sampling variance — it is an
attractor.

Prevalence, from every episode's stored root requests (`traces/blobs/*/step-*.root_request_ref.blob`):

| root | episodes with root turns | max identical run ≥ 10 | ≥ 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| MTP (S4, Aug 17–18) | 90 | **0** (longest: 3) | 1 |
| DFlash2 (this run) | 66 scanned at the time of analysis, 90 total | **2** (70, 111) | 4 |
| DFlash2 (other runs, Aug 20: smoke + the stopped 5-arm run) | — | **2 more** (77 in an `rlm` smoke episode on code QA; 135 in a restricted-arm episode) | — |

Every verbatim-repetition loop on record post-dates the drafter swap; none
exists under MTP. That is strong but not proof (n is small, and the two runs
differ in more than the drafter: one arm vs four interleaved, a different
llama.cpp build on the root). The discriminating test is cheap and is owed
before this finding is closed either way:

1. **Replay A/B — DONE 2026-08-21, revised after adversarial review
   (`s2/REPLAY-LOOP-AB.md`).** The exact rendered requests at onset /
   first-repeat / established points of both loops, replayed N=20 seeds each
   under five arms (production DFlash2, MTP on the same build, no speculation,
   S4's actual b10375+MTP binary, a DFlash2 replicate). **Repetition given the
   state is not the drafter's:** 61 / 65 / 67 / 65 / 60 of 120, Fisher p = 0.70
   vs MTP and 0.52 vs unspeculated; onset ≈ 6 % → first repeat ≈ 64 % →
   established ≈ 92 %, `final_answer` 0/20 once established, in every arm.
   MTP is distribution-preserving seed-for-seed (120/120 identical replies
   across two binaries, 102/120 vs unspeculated). **Entry into the state is
   NOT settled and the evidence points the other way:** DFlash2 diverges from
   the unspeculated path on 65/120 same-seed replies with a replicated bias
   toward shorter replies (44/21, p = 0.006; replicate 43/23), and the traces
   show the loop's entry condition (prose-free cell → empty observation) in
   **1/90 S4-MTP episodes vs 11/88 DFlash2 episodes (p ≈ 0.002), all in code
   QA**, where prose fell 47 % → 30 %; the other categories are identical.
   So "S4's 0/90 was chance" is withdrawn. DFlash2 stays on R4's stated
   criteria, but R4 now carries an owed **entry-rate A/B** (code QA × 3 seeds,
   `dflash` vs `mtp`, same day, interleaved) before "success unchanged" is
   clean. The review also found two scaffold-side contributors present in both
   runs: every past assistant turn is rendered with a doubled empty think
   block, and the root is sampled with the same seed on every turn of an
   episode (which turns a 64–92 % per-turn repeat into the 70/70 and 111/111
   observed). Both belong in the same spec amendment as the guard.
2. **Scaffold-side repetition guard, regardless of cause.** A root loop costs
   1,154–1,308 s today. C5 should treat *N identical consecutive cells* the way
   it treats a missing cell: one scaffold observation naming the repetition and
   pointing at `final_answer`, then a named terminal outcome (`repetition_loop`)
   rather than `context_exhausted`/`budget_kill` twenty minutes later. This is
   I1-clean (scaffold-owned termination) and must land as a spec amendment
   (§5 C5, §6 outcome enum) with a version bump, not as a quiet patch.
