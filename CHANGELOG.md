- **v0.3.20 — 2026-08-23. `-ctxcp 0` IS A VULKAN-ONLY LEVER: it breaks the shipped leaf geometry on ROCm, and it is also what makes Vulkan fast. On Vulkan the flag is harmless (14/14). **Incidental but load-bearing: `servers.bench_leaf` ships without `--cont-batching` and served B1 and B3 in S4 — the p = 1.0 row says that is harmless**, which was not previously known. **(2) A METHOD DEFECT, RECORDED BECAUSE IT NEARLY SHIPPED A WRONG ATTRIBUTION.** The harness's `--extra-flags` override replaces the flag list wholesale, so the first ROCm arm silently dropped `--cont-batching` and varied two things at once. Vulkan's leaf decode has never been measured at any depth; R14's Vulkan arm covers only concurrency 1 and 2; and no window between 640 and 1,024 has ever been sampled on either backend. ~40 minutes of box time. Moving the leaf remains a §9 gate decision and a §8 benchmark event.
